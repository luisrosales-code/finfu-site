What it does for you

PDFs or screenshots from your bank: it builds your accounts, cards, installment purchases and balances without you typing anything.

Records your purchases on its own

From your bank's alerts: notifications on Android, Apple Pay on iPhone or your bank's emails. Anything it cannot record without guessing waits for you to decide.

Your cards under control

How much to pay on each card and by when to avoid interest, and which card is best for each purchase.

An assistant that gets you

Budgets and goals

Know what you will have left at month end, when each installment plan ends and how much you saved.

Alerts before each payment

We let you know before a card is due or if you will not have enough to pay it.

How do I subscribe?
In the app, once you finish setting up your account, or in Profile → Manage subscription. On iPhone you pay through the App Store, on Android through Google Play and on the web by card (Stripe).

Can I use my plan on several devices?
Yes. Your plan belongs to your Finfu account: sign in with the same email on your phone or on the web. It is managed where you bought it.

How do I cancel?
iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Web: in the app, Profile → Manage subscription. You keep your plan until the paid period ends.

How do I restore my purchase?
On the plans screen tap "Restore purchases", with the same Apple or Google account you paid with.

How do bank alerts work on iPhone?
iPhone does not let one app read another app's notifications. In Profile → Settings → Bank alerts you will find two step-by-step paths: a shortcut that records each Apple Pay purchase and forwarding your bank's emails.

How do I delete my account?
In the app: Profile → Account → Delete account. Your data is deleted. If you pay through the App Store or Google Play, also cancel the subscription there.
