2.6 Local Import and AI Document Reading

Processing depends on the flow you choose. Local import uses OCR and parsing on your device to prepare transactions for your review before saving. Local import settings remain on your device. In contrast, when you attach a document to the assistant or upload it for AI reading during account setup, the file is sent to our servers and the AI provider for analysis, as explained in section 2.7.

We do not connect to your bank, request its passwords or use open banking aggregators for these features. Alerts that record transactions automatically are a separate optional flow described in section 2.8.

2.7 AI Assistant

When you use the Finfu AI assistant, we process what you type or dictate and the account, transaction and budget data it needs to answer you. If you attach a receipt photo, screenshots or a statement with the paperclip, or upload your statements while setting up your account, the file is sent to our servers and read by an AI model to propose transactions. Proposed transactions are not saved as financial records until you approve them; messages and files are processed to provide the service and are subject to the retention described in this policy.

  • Dictation is turned into text by your phone's or browser's speech recognition
  • The AI model is provided by Anthropic, which processes the information only to produce the answer and does not use it to train its models
  • We keep your messages with the assistant so you can see them again, for up to 90 days after each message
  • You can delete your whole assistant history in Settings; it is also deleted when you delete your account

2.8 Bank Alerts

If you turn it on, Finfu AI records your purchases from the alerts your bank sends you. It is optional, you set it up yourself and you can turn it off at any time. It works three ways:

  • Android notifications: with the notification access permission you grant, the app checks the notifications you receive on your phone. It only sends to our servers those that carry an amount and describe a money movement (for example, "Purchase of $389.00 at OXXO with your card ****1234"); all others never leave your phone and are not stored. You can revoke the permission in Android settings and stop reading a specific app
  • Apple Pay on iPhone: if you create the Finfu shortcut in the Shortcuts app, each Apple Pay payment sends us the amount, the merchant and the card's name. We have no access to your notifications or to Wallet
  • Bank emails: if you forward your bank's alert emails to your personal Finfu address, we receive them with Amazon SES, read them to get the amount, merchant and card, and the email is deleted after 7 days. We only receive what you choose to forward

With that information we create the transaction in your account or leave it in an inbox for you to decide. Pending alerts are deleted after 30 days. Your address and your link carry a secret key you can change in the app. Reading these alerts does not use AI.

2.9 Optional web measurement with Google Analytics

On finfuaiapp.com we use Google Analytics 4 only if you accept optional measurement. It helps us understand visits, acquisition channels and general site usage. The tag does not load before your choice or when you choose “Necessary only”. You can change your choice through “Measurement preferences” in the site footer.

If you accept, your browser may send Google the page path, permitted campaign labels, language, browser technical information and general events such as opening the app. Google also receives the connection’s IP address and may use cookies to distinguish visits; we configure measurement cookies to expire after 60 days, without renewing their expiry on every page load. We do not send names, emails, account identifiers, balances, transactions, documents or conversation content to Analytics. Enhanced automatic measurement and advertising personalization are disabled; we do not use User-ID or Google Signals.

GA4 retention is configured to 2 months for event and user data, without resetting the period on new activity. Retention changes can take 24 hours to apply, and deletion follows Google’s processing cycle. These controls do not cover Google’s standard aggregated reports. Withdrawing consent stops new transmissions from this integration, but does not automatically erase previously received data or all cookies already stored. You can delete cookies in your browser and review your privacy options with Google.

See how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services and its privacy policy. Rejecting measurement does not prevent you from using Finfu.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your profile, financial accounts, transactions and budgets while you maintain your account to provide the Service. You can delete information in the app or request cancellation using section 7.4. Account deletion removes associated data from operational databases; it does not mean every copy disappears immediately.

  • Assistant conversations: expire 90 days after each message or record; you can delete your history sooner.
  • Setup and onboarding analytics: expire 120 days after the tracking record is created.
  • Forwarded bank emails: scheduled for deletion after 7 days. Alerts awaiting review: 30 days. Auxiliary alert records have retention periods of 2 to 90 days depending on their purpose; a confirmed transaction follows your account’s retention period.
  • Operational backups: a recovery window of up to 35 days; manual copies older than that period are removed by an hourly check. They are used for incident recovery, not to resume commercial use of data you asked us to delete.
  • Technical operation and security logs: configured retention of up to 90 days after each event; some logs use shorter periods.
  • Operator tax records: receipts, invoices and records needed to meet Finfu’s tax obligations are generally retained for five years from the date the related tax return was filed or should have been filed, under article 30 of Mexico’s Federal Tax Code. Extended tax effects, appeals, litigation or another longer legal retention obligation may change the calculation or applicable period. This does not impose five-year retention on all your personal financial transactions.

Expiration and deletion cycles run asynchronously. Physical deletion may therefore occur after the expiration date; the configured period is not a promise of deletion at an exact second. Legal exceptions are limited to the necessary data and duration. Payment records that Apple, Google or Stripe retain for their own obligations are also subject to their policies.

AI processing: Anthropic’s standard commercial API policy provides for deletion of inputs and outputs within 30 days of receipt or generation. Longer retention may apply for legal or safety reasons: its policy provides for up to two years for content flagged for potential violations and up to seven years for safety classification scores. Deleting history in Finfu does not immediately remove those provider copies. We do not promise zero data retention. See Anthropic’s commercial retention policy.
